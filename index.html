The idea

Both sides start from the video prior.

Latent action methods differ in where a pretrained prior enters the pipeline. O²LaF initializes both the inverse and the forward dynamics from the same pretrained video DiT, and adapts each to its own role.

Interpretability · Action token

What action tokens look at.

Each action query qi reads the video tokens of two adjacent frames, xi and xi+1, in a single softmax; its final state hi is read out as the latent action zi. Below are two raw attention heads per clip: no averaging over heads or blocks, no debiasing. The heat stays on what changes between the two frames, such as the character, the hands or the robot arm, and the background stays dark.

Extension · IDM prior

How other video priors behave.

Our framework is prior-agnostic on the inverse side as well. Swapping the IDM to V-JEPA 2 probes slightly better, yet its actions leak source color and light into the target scene—showing that a strong probe alone is not enough.

Applications

Two dynamics, two tools.

Without action labels during pretraining, the learned dynamics and latent space generalize directly to downstream perception and policy evaluation.

01 · IDM · Motion annotation

IDM as a motion annotator.

The self-supervised IDM learns latent actions from unlabeled video. Can it provide a better foundation for fine-grained motion understanding? Train a small MLP head on them with CameraBench labels, and it tags camera motion far better than a 72B vision-language model.

02 · FDM · Policy verification

FDM as a policy verifier.

Frontier AI models are quickly becoming capable robot agents, increasingly built and tested in simulators. But a simulator only approximates the real scene, and real-robot trials are slow and costly. Our FDM offers a cheap preview across this gap: starting from a real photo, it imagines what a candidate action sequence will do in the real scene, before real-world deployment.

FAQ

Questions.

Does O²LaF use any action labels?

No. Both dynamics are trained on raw video. Labels appear only in evaluation and applications.

Can O²LaF use other video priors?

Yes. We test ST-Transformer, VideoChat3, V-JEPA 2 and Wan2.2 as the IDM. V-JEPA 2 probes best (88.62% vs 87.95%), but carries source color and light into new scenes, whereas Wan2.2 keeps the target scene. Our guess: starting both sides from the same prior gives them aligned internal representations, so the latent actions fit the FDM more naturally and carry less appearance.

Is the gain just a bigger model?

Adapting the prior matters more than size. A frozen Video DiT with only a trained head does much worse than LoRA, and rank-16 LoRA already comes close to rank 128. Keeping a 1.3B IDM fixed and growing only the FDM from 1.3B to 14B still improves decodability, so stronger predictive supervision helps on its own.

Why train the FDM at high noise?

At mid noise the noisy target still shows most of the answer, so the FDM can predict the future without using the action. Near pure noise it has to rely on z. In our sweep the best point sits around tFDM = 0.98; pure noise is slightly worse.

Why causal joint prediction instead of autoregression?

Autoregression sees clean past frames, and bidirectional attention sees noisy future frames. Both are shortcuts around the action. Causal joint prediction removes both and probes best: 87.95% vs 83.71% (bidirectional) and 70.13% (autoregressive) on first-person MIND.
